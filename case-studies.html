Portfolio

A selection of projects where BICP helped engineering teams communicate complex infrastructure designs through real-time 3D.

Benghazi Ring Road Digital Twin

Highway · Real-Time

Inputs: Civil 3D alignments · DWG · DEM · 3D models

We built a UE5-based real-time environment for a major ring road project, including interchanges, structures, and terrain. The digital twin was used for client presentations and design review.

  • Full 3D overview of road and junctions
  • Cinematic flythroughs for stakeholders
  • Optimized performance on RTX GPUs

GISTANBUL Urban Mobility Visualization

City · GIS

Inputs: Istanbul GIS corridors · HD orthomosaics · live mobility APIs

GISTANBUL blends Istanbul's citywide GIS layers, aerial survey and mobility KPIs into an explorable twin for corridor planning.

  • Metrobus, tram and ferry networks rendered on accurate terrain
  • Heatmaps for congestion, ridership and land use
  • Shareable video + web viewer packages for stakeholders

Ankara Smart Interchange Simulation

Interchange · Simulation

Inputs: IFC bridges · Synchro outputs · aerial scans

We visualized an intelligent interchange construction sequence with live traffic behavior, structural phasing, and quality-of-service analytics in UE5 for Ankara’s highway authority.

  • Lane-by-lane traffic playback synchronized with signals
  • Time-based construction and detour phasing
  • Sensor overlays for LOS and queue visualization
