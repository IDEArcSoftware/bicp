About BICP

BICP combines civil engineering knowledge, BIM/GIS expertise, and real-time graphics to build infrastructure-focused digital twins.

Who We Are

BICP is a visualization and digital twin initiative backed by IDEArc, Mynd and LXIR. We specialize in real-time infrastructure visualization and digital twin solutions for complex engineering projects.

Our Vision

To become the reference platform for infrastructure digital twins, enabling engineers, municipalities, and communities to interact with complex projects in a clear, intuitive way.

What We Care About

  • Engineering precision in every visualization.
  • Technical excellence and honest performance metrics.
  • Transparent collaboration with partner offices and clients.
  • Long-term maintainability of data, scenes and workflows.
